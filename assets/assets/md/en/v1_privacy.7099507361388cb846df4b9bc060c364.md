# Privacy Policy

JKAI LLC (hereinafter referred to as the “Company”) complies with applicable data protection laws and internationally recognized principles to safeguard users’ rights and freedoms. The Company processes personal data lawfully and transparently, and takes appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure security. This Policy describes the procedures and standards for processing personal data and provides guidance on how related inquiries will be handled promptly and effectively.

## 1. Purpose of Processing, Data Items, Retention and Use Period
The Company collects and processes only the minimum personal data necessary to provide the Service, and does not use it for purposes other than those disclosed. If the purpose or data items change, prior consent will be obtained.

| Category | Purpose of Collection/Processing | Data Items | Retention Period |
|---|---|---|---|
| Membership Registration (Social Login) | Member identification and management; customer support; prevention of fraudulent or unauthorized use; provision of advertising information and participation opportunities (with consent); provision/cancellation of ancillary services; compliance with legal obligations | [Required] Social login identifiers (provider/user unique ID), authentication/session tokens, email | Destroyed immediately upon member withdrawal or fulfillment of purpose. *Tokens destroyed upon expiration/logout. *If retention is required by law, the statutory period applies. |
| Step Count | Mission participation and reward provision | [Optional] Step count data | Destroyed immediately upon withdrawal or fulfillment of purpose |
| Reward Fulfillment (Digital Vouchers/Codes) | Delivery of event/mission rewards and eligibility verification (if necessary) | Name, contact information (email/phone), minimum data necessary for delivery/confirmation (e.g., delivery logs, receipt status) | Retained until completion of reward fulfillment and response period for errors/claims, then destroyed or anonymized |

If additional data collection is required by law, the Company will provide prior notice of the purpose, data items, and retention period, and obtain consent where necessary.

## 2. Provision of Personal Data to Third Parties
The Company does not sell personal data. Personal data may be provided to third parties only in the following cases, and only to the minimum extent necessary. Prior notice and consent procedures required by law will be followed.  
- With prior consent of the user  
- As required by law or lawful requests from government/judicial authorities  
- Where essential for service performance or operation (e.g., reward fulfillment, advertising/marketing networks or SDKs, cloud, security/fraud prevention)  
- Where necessary to protect the rights or safety of the Company, users, or third parties  

**Categories of recipients:** Reward fulfillment partners; advertising/marketing networks or SDKs; analytics/attribution partners; monitoring/log analysis partners; cloud and security service providers.  
**Cross-border transfers:** Data may be transferred to, stored in, or accessed from outside the user’s country (e.g., the United States, Singapore). Appropriate safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses), encryption, and access controls are applied. Where required, prior notice and consent will be obtained.  
**Minimum provision and notice of change:** Data provided is limited to the minimum necessary; material changes to recipients/purposes will be announced in advance and new consent obtained if necessary.

## 4. Retention and Destruction of Personal Data
**Principle:** Destroy without delay when retention periods expire, purposes are achieved, consent is withdrawn, or long-term inactivity occurs.  
**Procedure:** Deleted following internal approval; if temporary retention is necessary, isolate in separate storage until resolved.  
**Method:** Permanently delete electronic data; shred/incinerate paper; delete backups by periodic overwrite or separate procedures.

## 5. Rights of Data Subjects and How to Exercise Them
**Rights:** Access/copies; rectification/deletion; restriction/objection; portability (where applicable); withdraw consent and opt out of marketing; object to automated decision-making (where applicable).  
**How to exercise:** In-app/web settings, support, or email (privacy@xxx.com). Reasonable additional info may be requested to verify identity; representatives must provide proof.  
**Processing:** Requests handled without undue delay; may be restricted to protect others’ rights or comply with retention obligations.  
**Account deletion:** [Settings] > [Account] > [Delete Account]. Data required by law may be retained.  
**Marketing/Ads:** Change consent in app settings; device settings allow resetting ad IDs or limiting tracking.

## 6. Safeguards for Personal Data
- **Administrative:** Internal plans; processing logs; least privilege and separation of duties; training; processor supervision  
- **Technical:** Encryption at rest/in transit; access controls; MFA for admin accounts; security monitoring/logging; regular scans/updates; backup/recovery  
- **Physical:** Access control to server rooms/storage; secure media handling and disposal  
- **Incident response:** Detect-assess-contain-recover; notify without undue delay where required; prevent recurrence

# Privacy Policy

## 1. Purpose, Collection Items, and Retention Period of Personal Information Processing

JKAI LLC (hereinafter referred to as the "Company") processes personal information essential for service use for the following purposes. Personal information is not used for purposes other than those stated below, and if the processing purpose and items change, prior consent from users will be obtained.

### Membership Registration
- **Collection/Processing Purpose**: Member identification and management, customer consultation and complaint handling, prevention of fraudulent use and unauthorized access, provision of advertising information and participation opportunities, provision of additional services and benefits (limited to service subscribers), other purposes stipulated by law
- **Collection/Processing Items**: Profile information [Required] Gender, birth year, birthday
- **Usage Period**: Immediately destroyed upon contract termination (membership withdrawal) (however, if there are separate provisions under relevant laws, that period shall be followed)

### Step Count
- **Collection/Processing Purpose**: Step count mission participation, reward payment
- **Collection/Processing Items**: [Optional] Step count data
- **Usage Period**: Immediately destroyed upon contract termination (membership withdrawal) (however, if there are separate provisions under relevant laws, that period shall be followed)

### Winner Information Input
- **Collection/Processing Purpose**: Prize payment
- **Collection/Processing Items**: Name, phone number, account number
- **Usage Period**: Immediately deleted after prize payment

### Winner Information Input (Income Reporting)
- **Collection/Processing Purpose**: Prize income reporting
- **Collection/Processing Items**: Social security number
- **Usage Period**: Immediately deleted after income reporting processing

## 2. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

The Company processes personal information of data subjects only within the scope specified in the processing purposes, and provides personal information to third parties only in cases corresponding to Articles 17 and 18 of the Personal Information Protection Act, such as consent of the data subject or special provisions of law. Otherwise, the Company does not provide personal information of data subjects to third parties.

The Company provides personal information only to the minimum extent necessary with the consent of the data subject for smooth service provision.

## 3. Consignment of Personal Information Processing

The Company consigns personal information processing tasks as follows for smooth personal information processing:

- **Consignee**: Thewoogi Technology Co., Ltd.
- **Consigned Work Content**: Gift card delivery
- **Retention and Usage Period**: Deleted upon completion of delivery

## 4. Destruction of Personal Information

The Company destroys personal information without delay when it becomes unnecessary, such as when the retention period expires or the processing purpose is achieved.

## 5. Rights and Obligations of Data Subjects and How to Exercise Them

Data subjects may exercise their rights to request access, correction, deletion, and suspension of processing of personal information from the Company at any time.

## 6. Personal Information Protection Officer

The Company designates a Personal Information Protection Officer as follows to take overall responsibility for personal information processing and to handle complaints and remedy damages related to personal information processing:

- **Personal Information Protection Officer**: Representative of JKAI LLC
- **Contact**: privacy@jkai.com

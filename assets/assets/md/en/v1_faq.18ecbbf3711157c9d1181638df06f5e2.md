# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## How do I receive prize money after winning the lottery?

Prize money is automatically credited to your in-app wallet, and you can withdraw it to your bank account after identity verification.

## When can I receive prize money and rewards?

They are paid within 3-5 days after the winning announcement. For physical prizes, additional time may be required for shipping.

## If there are multiple 1st place winners, how much does each person receive?

The 1st place prize money is divided equally among the winners. For example, if the 1st place prize is 1 billion won and there are 2 winners, each receives 500 million won.

## What is LOTO6?

LOTO6 is a Japanese lottery game where you select 6 numbers from 1 to 43.

## My step count is not being reflected

Please check your health app connection. Go to Settings > Health App Connection and allow permissions for proper step count tracking.

## Can tickets received in one round only be used for that specific round?

Yes, that's correct. Tickets received for each round can only be used for that specific round and do not carry over to the next round.

## When can I enter the lottery?

You can enter from Monday to Saturday 8:00 PM each week. The draw takes place every Saturday at 8:45 PM.

## How are taxes handled?

Withholding tax (22%) is automatically deducted from prize money before payment. You don't need to pay separately.

# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## How do I receive my prize after winning a StepPot draw?

The prize will be sent to the GCash account you provided when you won, and you can use it immediately after payment.

## When will I receive the prize?

Prizes are paid within 10 days after the announcement of winners.

## If there are multiple first-place winners, how is the prize divided?

The first-place prize is equally divided among the winners.  
For example, if the first prize is ₱40,000 and there are 2 winners, each will receive ₱20,000.

## What does the Mega Lotto 6/45 draw at 9:00 PM on Wednesdays mean?

Mega Lotto 6/45 is drawn regularly every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.  
Among these, only the winning numbers announced on Wednesday at 9:00 PM are used to determine the first-place winner.  
Therefore, the Monday and Friday results are not used, and only Wednesday’s draw is valid.

## My steps are not being tracked. What should I do?

Please check your health app connection.  
Go to **Settings > Connect Health App** and allow permissions to ensure proper tracking.

## Can I use tickets from one round for another round?

No, tickets can be used at any time regardless of the round.  
However, please note that each round has its own deadline.

## When can I enter the lottery?

Entries are open every week from Thursday 12:00 PM to Wednesday 8:00 PM (PHP, GMT+8).  
The draw takes place every Wednesday at 9:00 PM.

## How are taxes handled?

Winners are responsible for paying their own taxes.  
We pay the full prize amount without deductions, and you must handle tax payments separately.

## Are lottery numbers generated automatically?

When using a ticket, you can either select your own numbers or choose automatic recommendations.

## How many times can I enter per day?

As long as you have tickets, you can enter without any limit on the number of times.

## Do tickets earned from missions expire?

No, tickets do not expire and can be used freely in any round.

## Where can I check the results?

You can check the winning status and detailed results on the home screen or in your entry history.
